# Sample Conversations

Use these examples to test the application and include screenshots of the results in the final report.

## Conversation 1: Latest GDP

**User:** What is the latest reported U.S. GDP?

**Expected behavior:** The assistant returns the latest GDP value, the observation date, and the latest year-over-year GDP growth rate.

## Conversation 2: GDP trend

**User:** How has U.S. GDP changed over the past 10 years?

**Expected behavior:** The assistant reports the approximate percentage change over ten years and explains that the chart shows the longer-term trend.

## Conversation 3: Core CPI inflation

**User:** What is the current Core CPI inflation rate?

**Expected behavior:** The assistant returns the latest Core CPI index date and the year-over-year inflation rate.

## Conversation 4: Relationship

**User:** What is the relationship between GDP growth and Core CPI inflation?

**Expected behavior:** The assistant reports the calculated correlation and explains that correlation does not prove causation.

## Conversation 5: Visualization

**User:** Generate a visualization comparing GDP growth and Core CPI inflation over time.

**Expected behavior:** The application displays the comparison chart below the chat interface.
